About the portal

Built so no student misses money they already qualify for.

India spends thousands of crores on scholarships every year. A large share goes unclaimed, not because students are ineligible, but because the information is scattered across dozens of state portals and central notices.

One catalogue

Central ministries, state portals (MahaDBT, ePASS, OASIS, e-Grantz), CSR programmes and family foundations, described in the same format so they are actually comparable.

One tracker

Every application filed here gets a submission ID and a timeline showing who currently holds the file — institute, department or sanction committee.

Zero fees, zero agents

No processing charges, no “guaranteed approval” packages. We show the official portal for every scheme and warn students about known scams.

How the catalogue is compiled

Each scheme is written from its official 2026-27 notification: the implementing ministry or foundation, award range, income ceiling, eligible study levels and the portal you apply on. Deadlines follow the annual cycle and are re-checked every fortnight.

What we deliberately do

  • Show the official link for every scheme, so you can verify before paying anything.
  • Keep the catalogue India-only — scholarships for study abroad are listed only when an Indian government body funds them.
  • Explain every application status in plain language, including what to do next.
  • Never store Aadhaar numbers, bank passwords or OTPs.

What we are not

Scholarship Hub is an independent student tool, not a government portal. Sanctions, disbursals and rejections are decided entirely by the scheme's nodal department. If our catalogue and the official notification disagree, the official notification wins.
