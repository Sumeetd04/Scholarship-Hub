Student handbook

Questions students actually ask

Short, practical answers. If something is still unclear, our support desk replies within one working day.

What is an OTR and do I need one?
OTR stands for One Time Registration. On the National Scholarship Portal it is a single profile — Aadhaar-linked — that every central scheme reuses, so you don't re-enter your details for each application. Scholarship Hub gives you its own OTR for tracking; you still need the NSP OTR for schemes applied on scholarships.gov.in.
Which income certificate is accepted?
A certificate issued by your Tehsildar / SDM / Revenue Department, usually valid for one financial year. Most schemes accept a digitally signed e-district certificate. If your family is salaried, an ITR or Form 16 may be accepted by private schemes. Typed self-declarations are not accepted for government schemes.
Can I apply for more than one scholarship at a time?
Yes — but most government schemes bar you from holding two scholarships of the same type. You can receive one central scheme plus one state fee waiver in many cases, provided the combined amount does not exceed your actual course fee. Always declare existing scholarships in the application form.
How do I track an application that wasn't filed through Scholarship Hub?
You can still use the portal's tracker only for submissions made here, since the submission ID is generated by us. For NSP or state portal applications, use the reference number from that portal on its own tracking page — our status glossary explains what each stage means.
My status says “Under Review” for three weeks. Is that normal?
Institute verification usually takes 7–15 working days, and department verification another 15–30. If nothing moves for more than 21 days, raise a query from the tracker page with your submission ID; we log a follow-up and show it on your timeline.
Why was my application rejected?
The three most common reasons are: income above the scheme ceiling, a caste or domicile certificate that does not match the application, and attendance or marks below the renewal threshold. The exact reason is always recorded in the officer remarks on your tracker page.
When does the money actually arrive?
After approval, sanctions are usually processed in fortnightly or monthly DBT batches. You will see Approved first, then Amount Disbursed when the bank confirms credit. Ensure your Aadhaar is seeded with the bank account and the name matches exactly.
Do I have to upload documents on Scholarship Hub?
Not yet. Submitting here registers your application and creates the tracking record; document upload happens at institute or portal verification, which the timeline will prompt you about. Keep scanned copies ready in PDF under 200 KB.
Is Scholarship Hub an official government portal?
No. Scholarship Hub is an independent, free student tool. It catalogues official schemes and helps you track applications, but final decisions rest with the scheme's nodal department. We never charge fees and never ask for your Aadhaar OTP.
What documents should I keep scanned?
Aadhaar, a recent passport photo, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, income certificate, caste certificate (with validity where your state requires it), domicile certificate, fee receipt, bank passbook first page and a bonafide certificate from your institute. Store them as PDF, under 200 KB each.
Can I edit an application after submitting?
If the status is still Submitted, contact support with the correction. Once it moves to Under Review, you cannot edit it; if something is wrong, the officer will place it On Hold and request a correction. Withdrawing and reapplying is possible only while the window is open.
Is there any fee to use this portal?
Never. Scholarship Hub is free for students. No government scholarship portal charges a processing fee either. Anyone demanding money to “guarantee” a scholarship is committing fraud — report them at cybercrime.gov.in.
