How tracking works

One submission ID. A file you can actually follow.

Most scholarship portals stop caring after you hit submit. This one shows you the queue, the officer and the next action.

01

Match

Your state, income, category and level are compared with every open scheme. The finder returns a score and the reasons behind it.

02

Apply

One prefilled form per scheme. Submitting locks your details and generates a submission ID in the format SH-YEAR-XXXXXX.

03

Track

Every state change is appended to a timestamped timeline — institute verification, document checks, sanction committee.

04

Get paid

When the amount is disbursed, the tracker shows the DBT credit and keeps the receipt available for your records.
